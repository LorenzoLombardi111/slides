Free Presentation Maker for Students

freeslides.app is a free presentation maker built for students who need slides fast without the hassle of creating accounts or installing software. Open freeslides.app in any browser — including on school Chromebooks and library computers — and start building your presentation in under a second. No login, no email, no signup. Just slides.

Why is freeslides.app ideal for students?

Students often need to create presentations on shared computers where they cannot install software or don't want to log into personal accounts. School libraries, computer labs, and shared family devices all present this challenge. freeslides.app solves it by running entirely in the browser with zero setup. There is nothing to download, nothing to install, and no account to create.

freeslides.app loads in under 200ms, which means students spend zero time waiting and all their time working on content. The interface is simple and distraction-free — no ads, no pop-ups, no upsell banners. Students see a clean slide editor the moment the page loads.

What features does freeslides.app offer for student presentations?

freeslides.app supports presentations with up to 50 slides, which is more than enough for class projects, book reports, science fair presentations, and thesis defenses. Each slide can have a title, body text, and speaker notes. Students can choose from 12 Google Fonts and three color themes to make their presentations look polished.

The built-in presentation mode lets students present directly from the browser without any additional software. Arrow keys, touch gestures, and on-screen buttons all work for navigation. Students can also export to PDF for submitting assignments as file attachments, or export to JSON for backing up their work and transferring between devices.

Does freeslides.app work on school Chromebooks?

Yes. freeslides.app is a web application that runs in any modern browser including Chrome, which is the default browser on Chromebooks. No installation is needed, no admin permissions are required, and no Chrome extensions need to be enabled. Students can bookmark freeslides.app and access it instantly from any Chromebook.

freeslides.app also works offline after the first load thanks to its service worker. Students who lose Wi-Fi during class can continue editing their presentations without interruption.

Is freeslides.app safe for students?

freeslides.app collects no personal data. There is no account creation, so no email addresses or passwords are stored anywhere. All presentation data stays in the browser's localStorage on the student's device. There are no ads, no tracking pixels, no analytics scripts, and no third-party cookies. Parents and teachers can verify this — the entire application is a single HTML file with no external dependencies beyond Google Fonts for typography.

How does freeslides.app compare to Google Slides for students?

Google Slides is excellent for collaborative group projects where multiple students need to edit the same deck simultaneously. Google Slides also integrates with Google Classroom, which many schools use. For these use cases, Google Slides is the better tool.

freeslides.app is the better choice for individual assignments, quick in-class presentations, and situations where students don't want to log into a Google account on a shared computer. freeslides.app is faster, more private, and requires zero setup. Both tools are free.
