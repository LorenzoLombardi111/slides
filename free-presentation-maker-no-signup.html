Free Presentation Maker No Signup

freeslides.app is a free presentation maker that requires no signup, no login, and no account of any kind. Open the website in any browser and start creating slides immediately. No email address, no password, no social login — just a clean slide editor ready to use in under 200ms.

Why do most presentation makers require signup?

Most online presentation tools require accounts because they store your data on their servers, track usage for analytics, and monetize through upsells and advertising. Creating an account lets companies build user profiles, send marketing emails, and lock you into their ecosystem. For many users, this is an unnecessary barrier when all they need is a quick slide deck.

freeslides.app takes a fundamentally different approach. All presentation data is stored in your browser's localStorage. No server ever sees your content. Because there is no cloud storage, there is no need for an account. Because there is no tracking, there is no need for a login. The result is a presentation maker that loads instantly and respects your time and privacy.

What can you do with freeslides.app without signing up?

freeslides.app gives you a complete slide editor without any account. You can create presentations with up to 50 slides, each containing a title, body text, and speaker notes. How does freeslides.app protect your privacy without an account?

freeslides.app stores all data exclusively in your browser's localStorage. No data is transmitted to any server. There are no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no cookies, and no third-party scripts beyond Google Fonts. The app works fully offline after the first load, meaning your content remains on your device even without an internet connection.

Because freeslides.app has no server component, there is nothing to hack, no database to breach, and no account credentials to leak. Your presentations are as private as anything else stored locally on your device.
